Questions people ask about this app

Where do I start reading the app?

Start in the centre with CRM Companion itself, then follow the orbit clockwise: Today, then Deal Detail, then Activity History, then back to Today. That is the same path a rep takes — a follow-up card opens Deal Detail, and Deal Detail links into Activity History for that deal.

Where does the data live?

All of it lives inside the single index.html file, in memory — the 6 sample deals and the 12 seeded activities are written into the file itself. Nothing is sent to a server or a database, and anything you do in a session is lost when the page reloads.

Where is the Call and Email logic?

In the Call panel and Email panel, which open from the follow-up cards on Today and from Deal Detail. Saving a call or sending an email moves the deal to Updated today and adds it to the Activity feed and Activity History. Closing a panel without saving counts as attempted instead — for a call once the timer has run or a note is typed, and for an email once the draft is typed or edited.

Why are Deals and Activity greyed out in the sidebar?

By design, for this test. The note under them says so: nav is off, and only the follow-ups on Today are in scope. Deal Detail and Activity History still exist — you reach them from a follow-up card, a row, or a link, not from the sidebar.

What is real, and what is mocked?

The behaviour is real: the screens, panels, timers, search, and states all work in the browser exactly as you see them. The content is mocked: the names, companies, amounts, and past activities are sample data, no real call is placed, no real email is sent, and nothing is saved anywhere.

Where does the loading, empty, and error wording live?

In the states of the two lists — the follow-ups list on Today and the activity list in Activity History. Each list can be loading (skeleton cards), empty, or failed, and the exact wording is shown in the state nodes on the orbit. Testers can force each state with the Data / Loading / Empty / Error controls above each list.

Tips for finding your way around

  1. 1Search, don’t scroll. Type a name in the search box — “error”, “email”, “data” — and use the result chips to jump straight to that part instead of scanning the whole orbit.
  2. 2Click a node for its connections. Every node’s panel has “Connects to” buttons. Follow them to walk from a screen to its panels, its data, and its states without getting lost.
  3. 3Follow the orbit clockwise. Today → Deal Detail → Activity History → back to Today is the rep’s real path through the app, so it is the safest order to review the build in.
